# Transit Hotel Policy (fictional, for a demo project)

## Eligibility for a complimentary hotel
Transit passengers receive complimentary hotel accommodation when the connection time between two flights is between 8 and 24 hours, both flights are on the same ticket, and no earlier connecting flight was available. This applies to all cabin classes. Connections shorter than 8 hours do not qualify.

## Meals
Eligible passengers receive dinner and breakfast at the hotel. Meal times depend on the hotel. Drinks other than water are charged to the passenger.

## Transit visa and leaving the airport
Passengers who leave the airport for the hotel need a valid transit visa or a nationality that is visa-exempt. The transfer desk checks documents before the hotel is assigned. Passengers who cannot enter the country may use the airport transit lounge instead.

## Shuttle and transport
Hotel shuttles leave from the transfer desk at the pickup time shown on the booking. If the shuttle has not arrived 30 minutes after the pickup time, passengers should return to the transfer desk, which will arrange a taxi voucher.

## Cancelling or changing a hotel booking
Passengers may cancel a hotel booking at any time before check-in by contacting the hotel desk with the booking reference. Changes of hotel are possible only when the new hotel has rooms available. Cancellations are confirmed by a staff member.

## Complaints and compensation
Complaints about a stay or transport should be sent within 7 days with the booking reference. A duty manager reviews each complaint within 48 hours. Compensation, if any, is given as travel credit.
